Marine Power & Hydrodynamics Calculator

Preliminary ship resistance, propulsion, propeller matching and rudder-stock screening using Holtrop-Mennen, ITTC 1957, Wageningen B-Series polynomials and IACS/BV rule-based rudder equations. All calculations run locally in the browser. Curated by Sadman Aznur.

Engineering-use notice. This is a preliminary design/checking tool, not a class-approved calculation. Holtrop-Mennen is statistical and its validity depends on hull-form similarity and operating range. B-Series is a model-series correlation. Rudder stock, steering gear, propeller cavitation, shafting and final engine selection require the applicable class rules and project-specific verification.
